3. Without an account: everything stays in your browser

The following is stored only in your browser's storage (IndexedDB, localStorage and sessionStorage) on your device. It is not sent to Escaque:

  • The games you import or play in Training, their analyses, the moves, the puzzles generated from them and your progress.
  • Your rewards: experience (XP) and level, streak and daily goal, and achievements.
  • Preferences: language, light/dark theme, sound, board, chosen coach and rival, your display name and avatar, and whether you have seen the welcome screen.
  • The Lichess or Chess.com usernames you link for importing.
  • The optional AI settings. The key is stored in this browser only if you tick "Remember the key on this device"; otherwise it only lives in memory until you reload. AI comments are kept for a while in sessionStorage so they are not requested twice. The key is never synced or sent to Escaque.
  • A copy of the app files (including the Stockfish engine, about 7 MB) so it works offline and loads faster.

Stockfish, the chess engine, runs on your device: the positions it analyzes never leave it.

Technical connection data. Like any website, when serving you the app our hosting provider (Cloudflare) receives technical data from each request, such as your IP address, your browser and the requested address. The app calls /api/me when it opens (to check whether you are signed in) and, to prevent abuse, some routes count requests per IP address or per user without storing them. Requests to /api are kept in Cloudflare's technical logs for a few days (3 on the free plan, 7 at most). We do not use this data to identify you or for anything other than running and securing the service.

4. If you create an account

Signing in is optional and requires being 16 or older. Before your first sign-in we ask you to tick a box accepting the Terms of use and this policy, including the transfer of your data to the providers listed outside Ecuador (section 6). Without that box no account is created.

What data the Escaque server stores

The server (Cloudflare Workers and the Cloudflare D1 database) stores:

  • Email address and display name assigned to you by Google or Microsoft (if the provider supplies them).
  • Your account identifier at the provider (the identifier Google or Microsoft gives Escaque) and which provider you used. We do not store passwords or Google/Microsoft tokens: they are only used for a moment to verify your identity.
  • Record of your consent: the version of the terms and of this policy you accepted, and the date.
  • Sessions: only a cryptographic summary (hash) of the cookie token, its creation date, its expiry and its last use (recorded at most once a day). At most 10 sessions per person.
  • Plan and subscription status: monthly, yearly or Founder; active, canceled, past due, expired or refunded; end of the paid period; and the customer and subscription or order identifiers at Polar.
  • Synced data, only if you have Premium (section 5).
  • Log of Polar notifications already processed (notification identifier, type and date), so they are not processed twice.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating and keeping your account, recognizing you when you come back and showing your plan: your explicit consent, given by ticking the box (LOPDP arts. 7 and 8; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Providing the Premium and sync service you purchase, and handling payment with Polar: performance of the contract (LOPDP art. 7; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Protecting the service against abuse (request limits, technical logs): legitimate interest in the security of the service (LOPDP art. 7; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects on you, and we do not build profiles.

How long data is kept

  • Account, consent record and plan: while the account exists. When you delete it, they are removed immediately.
  • Sessions: they expire after 30 days or when you sign out; expired ones are removed from the database in the next automatic cleanup.
  • Synced data: until you delete it or delete your account. If you stop having Premium, the copy is kept (so you get it back if you return) and you can still delete it whenever you want.
  • Processed Polar notifications: 30 days.
  • Cloudflare technical logs: a few days (3 on the free plan, 7 at most).
  • Backups: the database has a restore feature (Cloudflare D1 Time Travel) that keeps its history for some days (7 on the free plan, 30 at most). What you delete disappears from it when that period ends; we would only use it to recover the service after a failure.
  • Purchase records are kept by Polar as merchant of record, for the periods required by tax law.

5. Synced data (Premium only)

If you have Premium, the app uploads a copy of your data to the Escaque server so you have it on all your devices: games (PGN, opponents, Elo and dates), engine analyses, moves from Training games, puzzles and their progress (results, puzzle Elo and spaced repetition), rewards (XP, streaks, daily goal and achievements), linked Lichess or Chess.com accounts and import markers, your profile (display name and avatar) and your coach, board and rival-voice preferences.

Not synced: your AI key, the AI settings, the light/dark theme, the language and the sound. When you delete something that was synced, a minimal deletion marker (without the content) stays on the server so your other devices delete it too. There are technical limits on space (25 MB) and on daily changes per person.

6. Who data is shared with and international transfers

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Only these providers are involved, each for the stated purpose:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States, global network) — processor: hosts the app, runs the server (Workers) and stores the database (D1) with the account data. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google LLC (United States) and Microsoft Corporation (United States), only if you choose to sign in with them: they receive the sign-in request and send us your name, your email and an identifier. Their own policies and cookies apply on their pages (Google, Microsoft).
  • Polar Software, Inc. (United States), only if you buy Premium or manage your subscription. Polar is the merchant of record for purchases: we send it your internal Escaque identifier and your email, and Polar processes payment data (card, country, taxes and billing) on its own behalf, as controller, under its privacy policy. Polar uses Stripe to process payments. Escaque never sees or stores card data.
  • Lichess (lichess.org), only if you import from Lichess: your browser requests the public games of the username you type directly from Lichess.
  • Chess.com (api.chess.com), only if you import from Chess.com: your browser requests the public games of the username you type. If the browser cannot read the response directly, the request goes through an Escaque relay (/api/proxy/chesscom) that only forwards that public query, without your browser's cookies or other headers, and stores nothing; the URL includes the username and Cloudflare may keep the public response in its cache for a few minutes.
  • The AI provider you choose (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter or a compatible server you specify), only if you turn on the AI with your own key. Your browser calls the provider directly; Escaque never receives or stores the key or the replies. What is sent are the facts of the move: the position and the moves in chess notation, the engine's classification and line, the phase and the opening, your color, your approximate Elo (if you imported games with Elo), the language and the chosen coach; and, at the end, game statistics. Your name, your usernames and your full games are not sent. That provider processes the data under its own terms, which you accept when you create your key.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.): aggregated visit statistics, without cookies (see section 7).

International transfers. Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft and Polar are outside Ecuador (mainly in the United States), so creating an account means transferring your account data to those countries. The basis for that transfer is your explicit consent, given by ticking the box when you create the account (LOPDP arts. 33 and 55 onwards; for EU users, GDPR art. 49(1)(a)), together with the data protection commitments each provider publishes (for example, its participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, where applicable; GDPR arts. 45 and 46). Lichess, Chess.com and the AI provider only receive data when you use them, directly from your browser.

7. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Without an account, Escaque sets no cookies. If you sign in, two first-party cookies are used. They are strictly necessary and not used for tracking:

  • __Host-escaque_sid: keeps you signed in for 30 days. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax (JavaScript cannot read it).
  • __Host-escaque_oidc: temporary (10 minutes) while you sign in, as protection against forgery.

There are no advertising or third-party cookies in Escaque (Google, Microsoft and Polar set their own on their own pages).

The browser's local storage (section 3) is needed for the app to work. With an account, also: a copy of your plan (escaque:account, without the security token) so Premium works offline for up to 7 days, Polar's prices (escaque:products, 24 hours) and, in IndexedDB, the last-modified date of each record (changes store), used only for syncing.

This version does not use any analytics tool.

To know how many visits it gets and how long it takes to load, Escaque uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not build a visitor profile. It collects aggregated visit data (page, approximate country, browser, load times). If your browser or a blocker prevents that script, the app works the same.

By email

9. Minors

  • Escaque can be used without an account at any age, because without an account we do not collect personal data on our servers.
  • To create an account you must be 16 or older (the consent box asks for it). If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it; if you are a parent or guardian and think this is the case, write to us.
  • Purchases require being of legal age or having your legal guardian's authorization.

10. Security and breaches

We apply measures suited to the risk: all traffic is encrypted (HTTPS); session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite; the database stores only a hash of session tokens (if it leaked, they would not let anyone in); requests that change data require an anti-forgery (CSRF) token; server secrets are not in the code; and we keep the minimum data needed. No system is infallible: if a security breach affecting your data occurred, we would notify the Personal Data Protection Authority (within 5 days at most, LOPDP art. 43) and, if it poses a risk to your rights, you as well without delay (within 3 days at most, LOPDP art. 46; GDPR arts. 33 and 34).
